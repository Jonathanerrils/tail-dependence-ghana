## GenAI disclosure placement

The official September 2026 template states that substantive GenAI use should be disclosed in the manuscript and provides disclosure wording in the Acknowledgments guidance. The Risks wrapper therefore folds the ChatGPT disclosure into the Acknowledgments rather than using the `\useofartificialintelligence` command, which the template labels as Nursing Reports-specific.
