# Superseded — see `paper/latex/paper.tex`

This Markdown draft (the original working version of this project's
write-up) has been superseded by the full LaTeX manuscript at
`paper/latex/paper.tex` (compiled: `paper/latex/paper.pdf`), submitted
under the title *"Extreme Commodity Tail Risk and Exchange-Rate
Exposure in Ghana: Copula–EVT Evidence from Cocoa, Gold, Oil and the
Cedi."*

The LaTeX version differs substantively from this draft: it uses an
eight-family copula stack (this draft used four), a full family-wise
Bonferroni correction across all 60 tested cells plus an independent
Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction (this draft used a per-slice
correction), disaggregated COVID-only and 2024-only stress-window
results, a completed World Bank Pink Sheet roll-effect validation (this
draft listed it as not executed), and six independent robustness
treatments of the cedi's marginal distribution, including an explicit
hurdle model and a Markov-switching extension.

This file is retained for the project's history only. Do not cite
figures or conclusions from it; they are superseded by
`paper/latex/paper.tex`.
